[Meeting: Self-Checkout & Kiosk Modernization Kickoff]
Date: October 14, 2026
Speaker: Priya Mehta (VP Product & Customer Experience)

[00:01:15] Priya Mehta:
"Hello team. Our primary objective for the retail modernization program is to reduce average checkout time by 30% across all tier-1 flagship locations.

Key Decisions from Product:
1. Shoppers must be able to complete guest checkout without creating an account or logging in.
2. The user interface must support fast multi-item barcode scanning with instant subtotal updates.
3. Our launch window is locked to Q3 so we are fully stabilized before the holiday shopping rush.
4. Native mobile apps for customers are strictly OUT OF SCOPE for Phase 1. Development is restricted to in-store touch kiosks and supervisor tablets."
