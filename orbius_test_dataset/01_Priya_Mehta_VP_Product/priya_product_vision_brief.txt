# Project Brief: Next-Gen Retail Kiosk Experience
Author: Priya Mehta, VP Product
Status: Approved for Q3 Pilot

Executive Summary:
Customer drop-off during peak store hours is at 14% due to long cashier lines. The new automated kiosk solution will provide rapid self-service checkout while integrating with store inventory in real-time.

Target Metrics:
- 30% reduction in customer wait time.
- 99.5% completion rate for guest checkouts.
- Zero data loss during network disruptions via local basket queuing.
