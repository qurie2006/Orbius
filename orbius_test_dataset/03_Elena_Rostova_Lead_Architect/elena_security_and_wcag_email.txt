Subject: Mandatory Architectural Standards: 2FA, WCAG 2.2 AA and Offline Mode

Hi Team,

Please ensure the following three non-negotiable architectural requirements are implemented in the upcoming sprint:

1. WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility: High contrast mode, dynamic font scaling (up to 200%), and screen reader auditory cues must pass automated accessibility checks.
2. Store Manager 2FA: Any supervisor override action on the kiosk must trigger a 2FA mobile push notification or hardware YubiKey prompt.
3. Offline Queue: When internet drops, do not display an error screen. Queue up to 50 transactions locally in encrypted SQLite storage.
