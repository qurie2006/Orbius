[Field Operations Notes: Flagship Store #104 - Chicago]
Inspector: David Kim (Store Operations Manager)
Date: October 18, 2026

Observations & Requirements from Floor Staff:
1. Cashier override requests happen on average 42 times per shift (mostly age verification for alcohol and coupon adjustments).
2. Store supervisors need a lightweight tablet app to approve overrides remotely without walking over to every physical kiosk.
3. 78% of customers surveyed prefer digital e-receipts via SMS/Email rather than paper printouts.
4. Physical kiosk barcode scanner angles must be adjustable for wheelchair accessibility.

Operational Risks:
- Store associates cannot be fully staffed or trained before October 1st. Launching earlier in Q3 risks operational chaos unless pilot is limited to 2 stores.
