# Regulatory Compliance & Data Privacy Memo
Author: Amina Zaid, Chief Information Security & Compliance Officer

Mandatory Compliance Directives:
1. PCI-DSS 4.0 Compliance: Cardholder data (PAN, CVV) must NEVER touch local kiosk storage. Point-to-Point Encryption (P2PE) payment terminals must be used.
2. Privacy & GDPR/CCPA: Shopper phone numbers and emails collected for digital receipts must be purged after 30 days unless explicit marketing opt-in is granted.
3. Audit Logging: Every supervisor override and 2FA authentication attempt must produce an immutable audit log timestamped in UTC.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Pass external PCI-DSS vulnerability scan with zero high/critical vulnerabilities.
- Provide automated data retention purging job verified by automated test suite.
